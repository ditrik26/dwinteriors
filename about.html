DW Interiors is an independent interior design practice led by IDr. Ditrik D. Wieneke, operating at the intersection of international hospitality rigor, bespoke furniture fabrication, and high-performance residential planning.

Following his foundational training at the University of Santo Tomas in 2011, Ditrik directed residential and commercial projects across prominent architectural studios in Manila before joining Singapore-based firm DBTA International. There, he was part of the design teams delivering world-class hospitality properties across India for prominent international operators including Sheraton, Holiday Inn, and Park Inn.

In 2013, Ditrik earned his professional licensure with an elite 86.75 general rating, maintaining an active membership with the Philippine Institute of Interior Designers (PIID) ever since. To deepen fabrication precision, he pursued specialized training in sustainable furniture design at the School of Fashion and the Arts (SoFA), followed by a Master’s degree in Interior Design (MID) from Guglielmo Marconi University, authoring thesis research concentrated on behavioral spatial dynamics and customer satisfaction optimization in Food & Beverage environments.

Since 2018, Ditrik has served as a faculty lecturer in the Department of Interior Design at his alma mater, the University of Santo Tomas. This ongoing commitment to institutional design theory, manual drafting, and construction technology ensures that every commission undertaken by DW Interiors is backed by constructible contract documentation, precise material ergonomics, and uncompromising site execution.
